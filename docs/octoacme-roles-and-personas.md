# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality strategy and ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release. They collaborate with developers and product leads to define test coverage and validation approaches.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and acceptance criteria validation approach
- Plan and execute unit, integration, and end-to-end testing
- Identify and triage quality issues
- Ensure security and performance testing are completed
- Validate features against acceptance criteria before release

### Goals
- Ensure features meet quality standards and user expectations
- Reduce production defects through comprehensive testing
- Provide confidence in release readiness

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works closely with **Developers** to ensure testability of code and to provide early feedback on quality issues
- Collaborates with **Product Managers** to validate acceptance criteria and manage quality expectations
- Partners with **Technical Leads** to assess testing strategy and identify quality risks
- Supports **Project Managers** with test readiness reports and quality metrics for release planning

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and quality reviews
- Test reports and defect logs
- Pre-release readiness assessments

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical direction, review design decisions, and manage technical risks. They collaborate with developers and product leads to ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with technical standards.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design and architecture decisions
- Review technical approaches and identify risks
- Mentor developers and promote best practices
- Ensure code quality and maintainability standards are met
- Coordinate technical dependencies and integrations

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, scalable solutions
- Maintain code quality and system reliability
- Reduce technical debt and risk

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Mentors and guides **Developers** on architectural decisions and technical best practices
- Advises **Project Managers** on technical feasibility and risk mitigation
- Collaborates with **QA/Testing Leads** to ensure comprehensive test coverage and performance validation
- Works with **DevOps/Release Engineers** to ensure deployment architecture is scalable and reliable
- Partners with **Product Managers** on technical trade-offs and feasibility discussions

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review and guidance
- Risk escalations and trade-off analysis

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, prioritization guidance, and executive oversight. They approve project direction, allocate resources, and ensure alignment with organizational strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success criteria
- Provide priority and resource decisions
- Approve scope changes and major decisions
- Provide feedback and validation
- Escalate business-level risks and blockers

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value
- Maximize ROI and strategic alignment
- Enable timely decision-making

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Provides business context and strategic direction to **Product Managers** and **Project Managers**
- Approves major decisions and scope changes proposed by **Project Managers**
- Reviews progress and receives status updates from **Product Managers**
- Escalates business-level risks identified by the **Project Manager**
- Validates that delivered solutions meet business objectives in collaboration with **Product Managers**

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates
- Milestone reviews and approvals
- Escalation and decision requests

---

## DevOps / Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps and Release Engineers own deployment infrastructure, release automation, and post-deployment verification. They ensure reliable, safe transitions from development to production.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment infrastructure
- Plan and execute deployments to staging and production
- Develop and test rollback and mitigation procedures
- Monitor deployment health and troubleshoot issues
- Coordinate deployment windows and communication

### Goals
- Enable safe, reliable, rapid deployments
- Minimize deployment-related incidents and downtime
- Reduce manual effort and human error in releases

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with **Developers** to ensure code is deployable and CI/CD pipelines run effectively
- Collaborates with **Technical Leads** on deployment architecture and infrastructure decisions
- Coordinates with **QA/Testing Leads** to ensure smoke tests and post-deployment verifications are run
- Partners with **Project Managers** on deployment scheduling and release communication
- Supports **Product Managers** with deployment readiness and rollback procedures

### Typical Communication
- Deployment planning and coordination
- Release notes and deployment checklists
- Post-deployment monitoring and incident response
